Hey builders 👋 I shipped my entry for the Agent Experience Hackathon: Strands Decider.

*The Checkpoint* 🛂 is an AI border guard you have to outwit. Amazon Bedrock (Nova Pro) speaks the guard's words, and *Strands Decider 2B* makes every trust decision with a calibrated confidence. The suspicion meter you watch is literally that probability, straight from the model. Lie and contradict yourself and its confidence collapses on its own.

The reusable bit is a drop-in Strands intervention (`SuspicionGate`) you can put in front of any agent's committing tools, so a cheap calibrated call decides proceed / probe / block instead of a slow second LLM judge.

Fun detail: the demo voiceover is Amazon Polly neural, so the whole thing is AWS end to end. 🙂
